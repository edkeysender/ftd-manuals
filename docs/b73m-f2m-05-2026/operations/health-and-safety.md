FTD.aero Sp. z o.o. guarantees that use of this FNPT in accordance with the FSTD Manual in its latest revision, as delivered by the manufacturer with the device, is safe for all involved personnel. The FNPT is produced and prepared for operation in compliance with all applicable regulations.

Emergency stop buttons are available to the instructor on the IOS wall and to both pilots on the aft electronic panel wall; see [Emergency Stop Switches](/manuals/b73m-f2m-05-2026/operations/emergency-stop).

Fire protection is provided by a fire extinguisher available to all personnel inside the FNPT and a fire and smoke detector inside the IOS. Smoking is prohibited in the device.

Battery standby lights are installed for evacuation. In the event of electrical failure an external UPS provides sufficient power for 15 minutes of operation; after that time emergency lighting turns on to allow safe evacuation in case of further system degradation.

Internal communication with the maintenance room, or another external reference point according to the specific configuration, is provided. External access may be restricted by an RFID or code panel.

All dangerous equipment, evacuation paths and emergency exits are marked according to international standards. Mind your step when entering the FSTD; a metal ramp allows easy entry.
