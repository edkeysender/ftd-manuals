Emergency stop switches are fitted for both pilots and the instructor. They are red push-buttons with a yellow halo, easily reachable from the **pedestal panel** on the aft electronic panel wall and from the **IOS desk**.

Pressing any emergency stop removes power from all servomechanisms and drives in the trainer: flight controls, speedbrakes and autothrottle. All mechanical components can then be moved freely.

## Releasing an emergency stop

If a switch was pressed by accident:

1. Rotate the button in the direction of the arrows marked on it. The button pops out and power is restored to the servos.
2. Check the device state on the [Starting Panel](/manuals/b73m-f2m-05-2026/operations/starting-panel). Another start-up may be required.

## Related safety equipment

The IOS is also equipped with a fire extinguisher, a smoke detector with alarm on the ceiling, an intercom to the technician room, a first aid kit in a visible place and marked escape routes according to local regulations. A dummy fire extinguisher is fitted on the circuit-breaker wall for training purposes only and must not be used in an emergency.
