The FNPT is started and shut down from the starting panel, on the IOS wall above the IOS Lights panel. The panel is a two-state switch with corresponding visual indicators. Two indicators next to the knob show the device state: **NOT READY** and **READY**.

A touchscreen beside the knob reports the state of the software and carries the virtual shutdown button.

## Start-up

1. Confirm the UPS is online and its battery indicator shows it is powered.
2. Turn the knob from **OFF** to **ON**. The automatic start-up sequence begins for all simulator processes; no computer is switched on individually.
3. The **NOT READY** indicator stays lit while the device boots. This is normal and takes several minutes.
4. When the sequence completes, **NOT READY** goes out and **READY** lights up. The touchscreen shows the ready status message.
5. Set up the simulation from the IOS (see the IOS Manual).

:::note
Depending on the configuration of the device, the simulator computers are switched on in any sequence, or the main switches inside the IOS are pressed, once the UPS is online.
:::

## Shutdown

1. Confirm the training session is finished and the IOS scenario is closed.
2. Turn the knob from **ON** to **OFF**, or press and hold the virtual shutdown button on the touchscreen. The indicator changes from **READY** to **NOT READY** immediately.
3. The shutdown sequence is automatic and needs no operator action. It takes approximately **5 minutes**; **NOT READY** stays lit until it completes, and the touchscreen shows the shutdown progress.

:::caution
Do not remove mains power or press an emergency stop while **NOT READY** is lit during shutdown. Wait for the sequence to finish.
:::

If anything unusual happens during start-up or shutdown, contact the manufacturer before the next session to prevent consequential malfunctions.
